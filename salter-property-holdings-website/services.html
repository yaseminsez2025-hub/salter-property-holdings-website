Service 01

Lettings & Tenant Find

Getting the right tenant quickly, at the right rent, with the right paperwork — that is the foundation of a successful letting. We manage the whole process from first photograph to key handover.

  • Rental valuation based on live local market evidence.
  • Professional photography and compelling property listings.
  • Viewings arranged and accompanied at convenient times.
  • Full referencing — identity, credit, affordability and previous landlord checks.
  • Tenancy agreement, deposit protection and move-in handled correctly.

Full Property Management

The completely hands-off option. We become the day-to-day manager of your property: the tenant's point of contact, the rent collector, the coordinator of repairs and the guardian of compliance. You receive your income and clear statements — we handle everything else.

  • Everything in Tenant Find, plus ongoing day-to-day management.
  • Rent collection and monthly statements you can understand at a glance.
  • 24-hour reporting line for tenants — urgent issues acted on fast.
  • Scheduled inspections with photographic reports to you.
  • Renewals, deposit disputes and check-outs managed professionally.

Service 03

Rent Collection & Statements

Reliable income with the admin taken off your desk. We collect rent, chase arrears promptly and properly, and give you clear monthly statements — essential if you want the income without the bookkeeping.

  • Standing-order rent collection with prompt banking.
  • Proactive arrears management — early contact, firm process, legal steps if needed.
  • Monthly landlord statements with income, costs and balances itemised.
  • Year-end summary to make your tax reporting straightforward.

Service 04

Maintenance & Repairs

Small problems become expensive problems when they are ignored. Tenants report issues to us, we triage urgency, instruct vetted local tradespeople and keep you informed — with your approval required above agreed spending thresholds.

  • Urgent issues prioritised — leaks, heating, security dealt with immediately.
  • Trusted local tradespeople — vetted, insured and competitively priced.
  • Cost control — quotes obtained and your sign-off sought on larger works.
  • Works verified on completion, with photos and invoices filed to your account.

Service 05

Inspections & Inventories

Proper documentation protects everyone. We record the condition of your property in detail at the start, check it during the tenancy and document it at the end — so standards are maintained and disputes are rare and resolvable.

  • Move-in inventories with dated photographic evidence, agreed by the tenant.
  • Scheduled mid-term inspections — typically twice a year, with reports to you.
  • Check-out reports and deposit handling done by the book.

Service 06

Compliance & Safety

The legal landscape for landlords grows more complex every year. We track every requirement that applies to your property, diarise every renewal date and keep the evidence on file — so you are protected from day one.

  • Gas Safety (CP12) and EICR electrical reports arranged and renewed on time.
  • EPC valid and improvement advice where the rating requires it.
  • Smoke & carbon monoxide alarms fitted, tested and documented.
  • Deposit protection and Right to Rent checks carried out correctly.
  • How to Rent and prescribed information served and evidenced.
