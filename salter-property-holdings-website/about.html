Our Story

Established in Poole, focused on doing one thing properly.

Salter Property Holdings Ltd (company number 16940942) was founded with a straightforward conviction: residential property management should be personal, transparent and dependable. Too many landlords feel like a file in a drawer — we built this company so they never would.

Our registered activity is the management of real estate on a fee or contract basis. In everyday terms, that means we look after properties on behalf of their owners: arranging private rentals, sourcing and referencing tenants, collecting rent, coordinating repairs and keeping every property safe, compliant and well maintained.

Being in our first year of trading is not a limitation — it is our edge. We are hungry, accountable and personally invested in every instruction, because every landlord who trusts us helps build our reputation from day one.

What We Stand For

Four principles behind every property we manage

01

Transparency

Clear written fees, plain-English statements and no hidden charges. You always know what we are doing, what it costs and why. If something needs spending, we discuss it with you first.

02

Communication

Landlords hear from us before they have to ask. Tenants get quick, courteous responses. A named contact, a direct phone number and honest updates — that is how we work.

03

Care

A property is someone's largest asset and someone else's home. We treat it accordingly — with regular inspections, diligent maintenance and respect for both owner and occupant.

04

Compliance

Gas safety, electrics, EPCs, deposit protection, Right to Rent — the rules exist to protect people, so we treat them as the floor, not the ceiling. Every certificate is tracked and diarised.

Our Commitment

What you can expect from us

These are not marketing slogans — they are working standards we hold ourselves to on every property, every month.

Answered within 24 hours

Every landlord and tenant enquiry receives a substantive response within one working day — urgent maintenance far sooner.

Documented everything

Inventories, inspection reports, certificates and statements — kept on file and shared with you, so nothing relies on memory.

Compliance without gaps

Every legal requirement for your rental tracked, renewed before expiry and evidenced — protecting you, your property and your tenants.
